How you order

Four steps, same as on paper. Or grab a platter and skip the picking.

  1. Seafood

    Shrimp, lambay, mud crab, punaw, mussels, scallops (shell or meat), halaan, squid, rock lobster.

  2. Sauce

    Cajun, garlic butter, coconut curry, or Mama’s. Extra sauce is extra.

  3. Heat

    Original, mild, or spicy.

  4. Add-ons

    Rice, garlic rice, baguette, corn, marble potato, fries — plus grilled tuna panga, pork belly, fried tilapia, or steamed lapu-lapu if you want something off the boil.

DIY still comes with potatoes. Add bread and one order was enough for five.
