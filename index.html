The 50% is the deposit on that purchase order, not half of some future bulk order.

Do I need a new mold?

No. First orders should use an existing mold. You still choose colors, hook brand, and the pack. A new cavity comes later, if that shape sells.

How small can the first order be?

Hundreds of packs, not a container. Printed bags and new molds have their own minimums. We tell you which number applies before you pay.

Can I see it swim first?

Yes. We shoot tank clips — action, sink, pause — on the sample you would actually produce. If the clip is wrong, you do not pay for a production run.

What does “bait to door” include?

A signed sample, retail pack (bag, card, barcode, origin text), production to that sample, a check before ship, then a DAP quote to your address. Duty is a separate line. We are not your U.S. importer.

What baits do you actually do?

Bass and inshore: minnows, walkers, poppers, paddle tails, stick baits, shrimp-style soft plastics. Not frogs, giant musky, or wood on the first project.

Quality, who it’s for, how to start

Which hooks go on the pack?

BKK, Mustad, Owner, or Gamakatsu — the brand on the signed sample. We do not swap in an unnamed hook on the reorder.

What does quality mean here?

A locked sample plus a short list: hard baits (weight, balance, hardware, paint, pause); soft baits (shape, softness, oil, seal); packs (count, barcode, warning, carton mark).

Who is this for?

Creators, shops, and guides who already have a name — especially if a bait stocked out and the next run has to match. Not a sourcing agent, not Amazon white-label volume, not “cheapest China” RFQs, not brands that must be Made in USA.

How do I start?

Email a photo, or mail one bait. Photos get you a route. A mailed bait gets you a number you can trust. Say the shape, a target quantity, and whether you already have pack artwork. Write first if you want a mailing address.

Is the price the price at my door?

The quote is DAP to your address unless we write something else on the PI. Bond, importer of record, and unpaid duty stay with you unless they are on the PI.
